Manage your time

I feel sometimes how successful people around the world manage their time so well. On the contrary million other people like me struggle so hard. I really get perplexed. How is that possible. Same 24 hours, same 7 days a week, same 365 days in a year.  Still some by age of 25 gets double PHD’s, while others fails to do anything meaningful. Some by age of 30 gets busy running their own company and takes strategic decision while others spends more time cribbing about their current profession.  HOW successful people manage their time so well?

Let’s try to analyze how we spend our 24 hrs in a day. Around 7-8 hours of sleep, 1-1.5 hours of travel (for job or anything that helps to meet their livelihood), 8-9 hours of work and around 2-3 hours towards basic needs (food, bath, prayer, exercise, wealth management, health check-ups etc). This leaves just 3 to 4 hours for yourself. Yes most individuals waste this time. Literally. While more focused individuals use this time very effectively. That is why successful sportsman, entrepreneur spend 10 – 12 hours consistently.

Don’t they get tired? Don’t they get irritated with long hours of work?

I do get tired when I have  to work for extended hour. I do feel completely exhausted and tend not to anything concrete with the time I have for myself.  WHY?

I think the key lies in the word called PASSION and LOVE. Unless you feel from within that this is something you craved for your life and excelling on that is not a burden but a challenge that you want to take, you just cannot be HAPPY. Obviously talent will give that extra spark and push, but it is this PASSION that should drive you. There is another trick to it I suppose. A cricket batsman might be passionate about batting, but unfortunately to bat well you also need to do other important things (like physical fitness, concentration, agility etc) which would eventually help you to honor your batting skill. So along with the passion you also need to have DICIPLINE. If you have the passion and discipline, you will start achieving something in 10 years which other would take 30 years.

I think in the India context, 80% to 90% follow a profession which they have chosen out of circumstances rather than by choice. A person who likes reading Shakespeare and have passion in English Literature might be writing software program instead (In India literally everybody is in software):-). From the face of it, he might be surviving, but he will miss one thing for sure in his life. He will not be passionate about his work and always fail to manage his time well.

So I think whatever you do, please for the last time think are you doing something that you really LOVE. If the answer is NO, STOP, think how can you come out of this. If not drastic, at least plan when can you live your dream. Live your PASSION and manage your team well. Things will just fall in place automatically. Cheers…
