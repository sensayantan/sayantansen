January 16, 2017

IS AMERICA CHANGING?

Had stopped writing for a while now. May be 2 years. Well today i compelled to put my words again  against back drop of few back to back incidents. These incidents is forcing me to thing that may be AMERICA IS REALLY CHANGING. With new president joining the office very next week, hopefully i am fool enough to think the change is more evident due to his figure.

Let me explain 3 back-to-back incident first.

Went to Disneyland with my family [only major break this year with family]. Well due to my exhaustive travel schedule i do carry a highest status for my preferred hotels/car rentals/airlines. Bad on my part that due to this status, I am now very used to upgrades, bit of pampering, few kinds words here and there, few unexpected smiles from beautiful and smart receptionists/air hosts/hostesses etc. I know that without my status, no body looking at me ever give a decent respectful look. Yes I look quite ugly and not having a white skin definitely doesn’t help much at all. Anyhow coming back to my vacation. So using my points I booked this hotel [leading hotel chain in America, world-wide]. I get all necessary services and smiles while check-in. I also get my break-fast coupons. Didn’t realize then, at the time of check-in I receive the coupons for jus me and not for my family. As per policy, i am supposed to get coupons for at least one of my guest. Morning I go to breakfast, realize I am short of coupon and go to reception. There stands a very smart and elegant while young lady. I explain the problem. First thing she refuses to give me any coupon. Then after checking against my room number, the lady reluctantly proceeds to give additional couple and makes this statement “I have never seen a elite status member asking for additional coupons. I will arrange for additional coupon for your loyalty and since you are asking. Is there anything I can do?” I am shocked. This lady in just few words openly insulted me and I was shaking from within. Is this why I am in this country. I remember this country was (hopefully it is still is) so simple in showing minimal respect to everyone. Her words were absolutely not necessary. I know this country exactly the way Michele Obama explained in her probably last speech as first lady. Work hard, get good education, do your job sincerely, give best to your family, get respect and earn respect for what you are doing as you should be proud of your background, color, religion, profession as long as you are doing the right things. Am I thinking too much?

I might have stopped thinking about it or might have thought it was a one of incident and may be my enhanced expectation might be the reason for my disappointment, but here comes another incident. I am for my first official trip of 2017. So when you fly with highest status, usually you do expect upgrades every-time. Yes I am a normal human being who is greedy in its own way, plus I am ugly :-) Anyway so I get into my flight from San Fransisco to Atlanta and yes I do get a upgrade. Great. Happy. Year has started with a positive note. Getting into business class, I get even excited as I see seats go flat. So I can actually have a good 3 hours sleep and a full lunch (and drinks) before the sleep. Almost like a feeling of a king in its own way. Within 15 minutes of take-off, this beautiful elegant while air-hostess starts taking lunch orders. I am sitting at the almost last seat of the business class and hence able to see her while she is taking orders one after another from the front. I put my seat to almost flat position, put my blanket on, turn my TV on and wait for her to take my order. While watching I might have inclined my head in a rest position [later I realized that that was my worst move till that point in 2017 :-)] When she came close to my seat, she takes order on all 3 sides of mine but not mine. I thought I even had an eye contact for a second, but not sure though. Surprised. But you know what I thought may be she will come later, or worst case, me being an upgraded passenger (and not a real business class passenger who have paid extra buck), might have different arrangements. See these are worst case scenario thinking… you do think rubbish, but you do think.  So about 15 minutes later lunch is served to all, not to me. I was not in a mood to confront so I was about to get to sleep. Here comes another air-hostess. Yes she was older. Beauty of an old lady [which older guys doesn’t carry] is that with this category of human being you feel most comfortable to talk to. Always. Even she is mad at you, you can relate her pain, her madness. May be old ladies are easy to related to your mother? Anyhow coming back to story (:-)), she was very kind enough to ask and I told her that no-body took my order. She immediately apologies and goes back to check. She comes back after couple of minutes and she says the previous person found you sleeping and hence…. the obvious. This time I got bit angry. Because I remember the same lady actually almost shakes the guy right across me [I know I am exaggerating here] and saying that “sorry to disturb you, but we have to take your order now as otherwise we will not be able to serve your preference”.  Why she did not do the same to me, if I was really sleeping? I did realize coincidentally I am the only non-white guy around me. I wanted to confront. So I request to make me speak to that lady. This old lady on the way back also asks me what I would like to have. I wait for about 20 minutes. The old lady comes back with the lunch and says actually salmon salad that you ordered was not readily available. Actually i made sure my in-flight cook actually cooks fresh for you and hence the delay. Great, but I wanted to talk to the other lady. I think self. Where is she? I want to ask. But this is what the old lady says “Well she is on the other side and is not able to talk to you right now”… thats it. That long expected conversation never happened.  Why she didn’t come an speak to me? Who was worried here. Me or she? What was going on her mind? Is my skin color anything to do with it? Worst part my plan to sleep just got ruined. So I thought of my last try. When you off-board, usually you do meet most of air-hostesses and they greet you and thank you. unfortunately that lady made sure that she don’t come on my way at all. Again question to myself. Am I unnecessarily relating things the wrong way. Previous one was a verbal one and this one a silent…..

Here comes the last one. Same trip. I come back from client office and swipe in to my hotel room. See the room not cleaned. Checked if by mistake do-not-disturb tag was on the door. Nope. All correct. I do remember that while going to office, I did called the house-keeping to clean my room as I left my room after the scheduled time of their cleaning in the morning. I call the reception. A guy picks the call. I know how this guy looks (again young, tall white guy) because around this time, he is on duty (along with another lady). He is that kind of guys, to whom your first impression is he will not be easy. When he smiles, it will be like , “Man, my job is to smile at you, but if get a chance I will never look at you at all… you know my how much my smile is precious?”. Again I am exaggerating. He picks up the call. I share my concern. He tells me that he will call back. I waiting for 10 minutes and no call back. I call back again. This time he snaps. He says “What are you expecting from me? To clean your room now? You will have to wait for tomorrow. Maximum I can do is send as many towels you want and give you complementary water for the evening”. I am awe stuck. Before I can react, he says “Is there anything else before I can discontinue the discussion?”. I had to hang up. One thing to observe this time is that compared to previous incidents, this time this guys never knows who am I. He doesn’t know my color, how I look. Why he behaved this way? Is he feeling that this is my time. This is my world. Everybody else is no-body. Not sure. On the funnier side, I raise on-line complain and instead of someone from the hotel reaching me, I receive a email with marketing stuff and then a call from a call center (definitely from India) where this lady over the phone notes down all the details and my contact details etc. She never tried to ask me the name of the person. I remember the name of the person very well as it on his badge. Actually of all 3 incidents, third incident is more scary.

I have experienced lot good-to-have experiences here in this country, which I can be very proud of. Yes people here are usually very positive and you don’t feel that tension of racism while having any conversations with anyone (even stranger). Are things changing? Is rudeness, color, religion new criteria for good vs. bad, superior vs. inferior? I am confident it will not be.
