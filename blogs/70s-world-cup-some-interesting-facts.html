70's World Cup, some interesting facts ?

By Sayantan Sen, December 01, 2014.

While the world cup of 70's was dominated by the West Indies, there are actually some interesting facts for the 75 and 79 world cups which may have been forgotten due to West Indies dominance.

1975 World Cup

1st match of the WC was only the 19th one-day international and hence almost all team were almost new to the format.

·         Inspite of the new format, there were some big centuries in the tournament with the first match producing a century from DL Amiss (England) against India. There were total 6 centuries out of 15 matches with GM Turner (New Zealand) scoring the highest run of 171 against minnows East Africa (Sri Lanka were the other non-test playing nation)

·         WI Team arguably had the most formidable bowling attach out of all team. Bowling attach took 45 wickets (3 all-out effort) and 3 WI bowlers were the highest wicket taker of the tournament (out of 5 matches). Other than WI though Australia, England and Pakistan also had some good effort where out of 15 matches, 5 matches had bowlers taking the Man-Of-the-Match award.

·         Although Sunil Gavaskar was famous for his 36* against England, interestingly he had second highest average in the world cup after GM Turner (New Zealand). The number was staggering. Average of 113.00 in 3 matches with 2 not outs :-)

·         Clive Lloyd, MOM in the Final made his first signifinant contribution in the WC with a match winning 102

·         Sarfraz Nawaz (Pakistan) was the first person in a WC history to win a MOM award from the loosing team. West Indies were 9-203 while chasing 267 and it was Andy Robert's batting heroic (24 not out) along with DL Murrey (60 not out) which helped West Indies to surpass Pakistan.

·         NZ, Aus, Eng and WI were the 4 SF'list and both the SFs were an anticlimax where the losing team batting first were bowled out cheaply and it is the bowlers who were the tormentors. In the first Semi Final, GJ Gilmour (Australia) buldozed England with his 6 for 12 effort and England were all-out with a score of 93 only. In the second Semi Final, New Zealand was bowled out at a mere score of 158 with BD Julian picking up 4, Andy Roberts 2 wickets and VA Holder 3

·         Final was a game of nerves for Australia, where while chasing 291, Australia were short of 20 odd runs and highest wicket taker was run outs (5 in total) :-). Viv Richard was the probably the popular Man-of-the-Match (Clive Lloyd was the given the MoM award eventually) as he was involved in 3 major run-outs, may be at that time giving a fielder a MOM award was something not as per the rule book.

1979 World Cup

This world cup was almost a mirror copy of the previous World Cup. Same number of matches (15 in total), same set of teams (Eng, Aug, New Zealand, West Indies, India, Pakistan, Srilanka (non-test playing) and Canada (non-test playing), same group structure (2 groups, 4 teams each and 2 Semi-Finals followed up with the Finals) and the same winner (West Indies) under the leadership of the same captain Clive Lloyd.

·          This was although the first WC where Rain-God had its impact and inspite of 1 day reserve for a one-day game, first match of WC was abandoned. It was a West Indies vs. Sri-Lanka match abandoned on Jun 13-15, 1979.

·         This was also the first WC where matches were decided on the second day. Actually 3 matches were declared on 2 days and one of them was the first major upset of the World Cup history where India lost to non-Test playing nation Sri-Lanka. This was on Jun 16-18, 1979.

·         Minnows Canada scored the lowest score in the World Cup history till then, when they were bundled out for 45 runs against England.

·         Unlike the first World Cup, Australia team was almost a club team as Australia handicapped themselves by selecting a largely unknown team (remember Graham Porter or Jeff Moss?) as their best players were still contracted to Kerry Packer's World Series Cricket. West Indies and Pakistan, fearing fractions at home if they fared badly, chose all their WSC players

·         It was second time in a row for West Indies, England and New Zealand to make it to the Semi -Finals, while Pakistan was first Asian country to make it to the Semi Final. First Semi-Final between England and New Zealand was the closest match of the tournament with New Zealand falling short by 9 runs only. GA Gooch (England) 71 was just right for England. England also had another very close encounter with Pakistan during the last Group Match when Pakistan fell short by just 14 runs. This match was the most interesting because Imran Khan came in 8th down, was 24 not out, but it was G Boycotts 2 last wicket which helped ENG to win

·         While the first World Cup final was almost a suicide attempt from Australia ( 5 run-outs), this final stamped West Indies dominance in one-day cricket. While Viv Richards 138* earned him the Man-Of-the-Match, but J Garner took 5 for 38 and 4 wickets fell in a span of 3 runs which broke England chase completely. This final although will always be remembered for Collis King blasting 86 for 66 balls. Just brilliant.
