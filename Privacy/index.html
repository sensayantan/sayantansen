Privacy & contact

Last updated 1 October 2026

The short version

  • This is Sayantan Sen's personal website. It sells nothing, shows no ads, and sells or shares no one's information.
  • It sets no cookies of its own and, today, runs no analytics.
  • It never asks for or stores a password. Where you sign in, you do it on GitHub's own page.
  • A message sent with the contact form below is emailed to me and not kept on the site.

When you visit

The site is hosted on GitHub Pages. As with any website, the servers that deliver the pages receive technical details of each request, such as your IP address and browser, to serve and protect the site; GitHub handles these under the GitHub Privacy Statement. The site's domain and its DNS are managed by Cloudflare.

The fonts are loaded from Google Fonts, so your browser contacts Google to fetch them, which shares your IP address and browser details with Google under the Google Privacy Policy.

There is no analytics on the site today. If visitor counts are added, they will be counts only — no IP addresses stored and no cookies — and this page will say so before they start.

Comments on blog posts

Comments today use giscus, which stores each comment as a public GitHub Discussion in this site's repository. Commenting means signing in with GitHub on GitHub's own page; your GitHub name, picture and comment are then public on GitHub, under GitHub's privacy statement. This site never sees your GitHub password.

A new comment system that lets you sign in with Google, Facebook, X or GitHub is planned. Before it goes live, this page will set out exactly what it keeps — your name, profile picture, the sign-in provider's user ID, the time and your comment — where that is kept, and how to have it deleted.

My Experiment's search

A question asked on My Experiment is sent to a small service on Cloudflare that searches published research and asks an AI model on Cloudflare to summarise the results. The question is not stored. To stop the service being overloaded, it counts questions per IP address for one minute, and that count then expires.

The contact form

The form below sends your name, your email address if you give one, and your message to me by email, through Cloudflare's email service. Nothing is stored on the site. Before sending, a Cloudflare Turnstile check confirms you are a person rather than a spam bot; Cloudflare processes that check under its own privacy policy. I keep messages in my inbox only as long as I need them, and will delete yours on request.

Admin and project pages

The /admin editor and the project documentation are for me alone. Signing in to them happens on GitHub. The project documentation keeps the sign-in only in that browser tab; the editor keeps it in that browser until I log out.

Links to other sites

Pages here link to other websites — the news publishers Daybreak cites, research papers, and social profiles. Those sites have their own privacy policies, which apply once you follow a link.

Your requests

To ask what I hold about you, to have a message or comment deleted, or with any question about this page, use the form below. I will reply as soon as I reasonably can. If this page changes, the date at the top changes with it.
